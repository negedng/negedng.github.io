## Overview

FindTheBeat is a music beat detection app that listens to music via your device's microphone, detects the tempo in real time, and suggests dance moves. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Microphone Access

FindTheBeat requires microphone permission to detect musical beats and tempo. Your audio is:

- Processed **entirely on your device** in real time
- **Never recorded**, stored, or saved to any file
- **Never transmitted** to any server or third party
- Discarded immediately after each audio frame is analyzed

## Advertising

FindTheBeat displays ads provided by Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK may collect certain data as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), including:

- Device identifiers
- Ad interaction data
- General location (IP-based)

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings under **Google > Ads**.

## Data Collection

FindTheBeat itself does **not** collect, store, or share any personal data. The app stores only your preferences (selected genre, theme, language, and dance move selections) locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data is never transmitted.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google AdMob**: Used for displaying advertisements. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Play Services**: Required for ad delivery.

## Children's Privacy

FindTheBeat does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
